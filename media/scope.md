# Your instance, one click away

**All environments** is the default. Select an environment from the Unleash sidebar when you want a narrower view.

**Try Demo** lets you explore with sample flags.

Click **Unleash Demo · Exit** in the status bar, or **Return to my instance** in the sidebar, to resume your connection. Switching to a real file resumes your connection automatically; returning to the demo tab shows the sample flags again. Closing the last demo tab ends Demo. No credentials to re-enter.

The sidebar also provides **Refresh flags** and **Settings and connection**. The **Flags** section lists all known flags; use its search icon to filter by name, project or description.
